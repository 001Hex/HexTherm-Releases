Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Hex Modifications

HexTherm is a Mac menu bar app that shows temperature and fan speed. It is designed to need as little from you as possible.

What HexTherm collects

Nothing. HexTherm has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry. Hex Modifications does not receive any information about you or your Mac.

Network use

HexTherm makes no network connections and contains no networking features such as an updater or analytics. HexTherm is not sandboxed (macOS does not allow sandboxed apps to read the System Management Controller), so you can confirm this yourself with a firewall such as LuLu or Little Snitch.

What it reads and stores

HexTherm reads sensor values (temperatures and fan speeds) from your Mac's System Management Controller and shows them on screen. These values are kept in memory only and are not saved or sent anywhere. Your settings (unit, refresh rate, thresholds, launch at login) are stored locally in your macOS preferences for the app, com.hexmodifications.HexTherm.

Permissions

HexTherm does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or notifications.

Downloads

The app is distributed through GitHub Releases and Homebrew. Those services have their own privacy policies and may log download requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
